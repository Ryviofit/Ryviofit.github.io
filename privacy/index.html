Privacy Policy

Developer and data controller: ARTICOM IMPORT OG INVEST JENSEN, a Norwegian sole proprietorship owned by Jan Trygve Berg Jensen, organization number 984 762 054. Privacy contact: post@aini.no.

Information handled by the game

PULSE//RUN does not require a player account. Game progress, unlocked content, scores, settings, daily-streak data, advertising cooldowns and local purchase-delivery records are stored on the device. Local data may be removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

The game does not use developer-operated analytics and does not ask for a name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or user-generated content.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for interstitial and voluntarily selected rewarded ads. Depending on region, consent choices, device and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process network and device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and advertising identifiers to provide, measure and secure ads and prevent fraud.

An active ad-free entitlement prevents ads from being loaded or shown. Where required, Google's consent form is displayed before ads are requested, and advertising privacy choices can be reopened from Settings. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes in-app payments under its own policies. To validate a purchase and prevent duplicate delivery, the game may send the store, product ID, signed receipt or purchase token, transaction reference and transaction date to Articom's secure purchase-verification service. The service does not receive full payment-card details.

Verification records are limited to what is needed to deliver entitlements, prevent fraud, meet accounting requirements and respond to store refunds. The final production service's hosting location and concrete server-log retention will be added to this notice before in-app purchases become publicly available.

Website hosting

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses and request information for security and service operation under its Privacy Statement.

Sharing and retention

Share Run opens the phone's system share sheet only after the player selects it. PULSE//RUN does not automatically publish scores. Local game data remains until overwritten, cleared or removed with the app. Advertising and store providers retain data under their policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and contact

Players can remove local data through the operating system's app-storage controls and can manage advertising choices in the game or device settings. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to post@aini.no. A store transaction reference may be needed to locate purchase-verification data.

Children and changes

The game is not directed specifically to children. This notice may be updated when features, providers or legal requirements change; the date above identifies the current version.
